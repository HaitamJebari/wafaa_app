About our counseling

A supportive space to work through life's challenges

Our founder, Wafaa Saad, has over 35 years of experience in anger management and mental health therapy.

Through individual sessions or a series of sessions, she works with clients to address personal conflicts, develop inner confidence and regain control of their lives.

Supported by a team of dedicated and optimistic associates, WAFAA provides guidance throughout the healing process, maintaining a confidential and professional relationship with clients.

Every step toward understanding yourself is a step toward a stronger future.

Our services

Counseling support designed around your needs

WAFAA offers different types of counseling and wellness support to help individuals navigate personal challenges and work toward positive change.

Anger Management

Explore the stages and types of anger, understand the mental, physical and emotional effects of uncontrolled anger, and develop greater awareness through empathy and self-care.

Short-Term Mental Health Counseling

Receive focused counseling support to explore immediate concerns, navigate difficult situations and work toward greater emotional clarity.

Long-Term Mental Health Therapy

Explore ongoing therapy support for longer-term personal and emotional challenges, with referrals available when additional assistance is needed.

Wellness Sessions

Make space for personal well-being, self-awareness and positive habits that support a healthier relationship with yourself and others.

Our approach

Guidance built around understanding and trust

We believe that every person's experience is different. Our approach focuses on creating a professional and supportive environment where individuals can work toward positive change.

Personal growth

Build confidence and self-understanding

Work through personal conflicts, explore emotional challenges and develop a stronger understanding of yourself and your needs.

Ongoing guidance

You do not have to navigate everything alone

With guidance from Wafaa Saad and the WAFAA team, clients can receive support throughout their counseling and wellness journey.

A relationship built on trust

Your journey deserves respect, care and confidentiality

We understand that reaching out for counseling can be a personal decision. WAFAA emphasizes maintaining a strictly confidential and professional relationship with its clients.
