Parenting Classes

Confident parents build stronger families.

At WAFAA, we understand the pressure that comes with parenthood, especially during times of emotional and financial stress. We are here to guide you as you enter parenthood, or when you run into bumps in the road as a current parent.

Apply for Support Ask a Question

What our classes cover

  • ✓Developing proper discipline and reward responses
  • ✓Maintaining cleanliness and hygiene
  • ✓Balancing work and childcare
  • ✓Addressing the physical and emotional stress of parenting
Our approach

Support rooted in real family life

Our shelters often see parents and their children come and go. While they are with us, we help them build the everyday skills that make home a calmer, healthier place for the whole family.

Our parenting classes cover those same topics, so every parent in our community can benefit from the guidance we offer in our shelters.

Strengthening families through education, to reduce isolation and build connections.
Topics

Four areas where parents tell us they need support

Each topic is practical and focused on what families face day to day.

Discipline & Rewards

Develop proper discipline and reward responses that guide children with care and consistency.

Cleanliness & Hygiene

Maintain cleanliness and hygiene habits that keep your home and your children healthy.

Work & Childcare

Find a workable balance between your job and the care your children need.

Parenting Stress

Address the physical and emotional stress of parenting, so you can care for yourself too.

Who it's for

Whether you are just starting out or already in the thick of it

Entering parenthood

Expecting or new to parenting? We guide you as you enter this new chapter, so you feel prepared rather than overwhelmed.

Hitting bumps in the road

Already a parent and facing a difficult season, perhaps with added emotional or financial pressure? You do not have to handle it alone.
