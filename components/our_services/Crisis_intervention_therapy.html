Helping You Navigate Difficult Moments

If there is an imminent crisis in your life, or the life of a loved one, that you believe needs an intervention, please reach out to us.

At WAFAA, we prioritize the safety and wellbeing of the people in our community. We provide assistance and resources intended to help de-escalate difficult situations and identify practical next steps.

Sometimes stressful situations can escalate and lead to unwanted effects. Understanding what is happening is an important part of finding a way forward.

Our support

How We Can Help

Every situation is different. We take time to understand the circumstances and work with the person seeking assistance to identify appropriate next steps.

Listening and Understanding

We begin by listening and gathering relevant background information to better understand the situation and the factors contributing to it.

Immediate Support

We work to identify immediate stressors and discuss practical ways to help reduce tension and address the situation.

Planning Next Steps

Together, we identify possible immediate and long-term solutions that the client can consider implementing after the session.

Our approach

A Thoughtful Path Forward

Our approach focuses on understanding the circumstances, addressing immediate concerns, and identifying solutions.

01

Share Your Situation

We gather background information and a timeline of relevant events and factors to understand the situation.

02

Private Session

A private session may take place in person or through available remote technology with the person requiring assistance.

03

Identify Solutions

Together, we explore ways to lessen immediate stressors and identify possible short-term and long-term solutions.
