Meet our founder Wafaa Saad Founder & community advocate
Our founder

A life spent
making room.

Wafaa Saad brings more than 25 years of mental health experience to WAFAA — and a deep belief that support should feel human, practical and close to home.

Her experience includes St. Joseph’s Hospital, WAFAA House, Women Rising Shelter, Strengthen Our Sisters and the Army Hospital in Alexandria, Egypt. She also serves through community groups, the Sheriff’s Department in Passaic County, Congressman Bill Pascrell’s office and the United Arab-American Coalition.

She shows up with humility, urgency and compassion for anyone facing a difficult chapter — and builds bridges so no one has to navigate it alone.

“
I fight with all my might for the cause of any individual in need.
Wafaa Saad

National Arab American Heritage Month

Celebrating heritage.
Recognizing contributions.

This White House message celebrates Arab American culture, heritage, and the important contributions Arab Americans make to the nation.

It also recognizes the dedication of organizations working to empower communities, strengthen connections, and ensure that Arab Americans have a voice and remain connected to their heritage.
