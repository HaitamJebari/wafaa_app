01Why WAFAA exists

Our mission is to strengthen families and the community through education in order to reduce isolation, build connections within and between families, and throughout the community.

The WAFAA promise

WAFAA is a nonprofit organization helping families and individuals through social programs that make support easier to find and easier to trust.

02Who we are

A life spent
making room.

Wafaa Saad brings more than 25 years of mental health experience to WAFAA — and a deep belief that support should feel human, practical and close to home.

Her experience includes St. Joseph’s Hospital, WAFAA House, Women Rising Shelter, Strengthen Our Sisters and the Army Hospital in Alexandria, Egypt. She also serves through community groups, the Sheriff’s Department in Passaic County, Congressman Bill Pascrell’s office and the United Arab-American Coalition.

She shows up with humility, urgency and compassion for anyone facing a difficult chapter — and builds bridges so no one has to navigate it alone.

“I fight with all my might for the cause of any individual in need.”Wafaa Saad

03Food pantry & shelter

Food is a form
of welcome.

WAFAA collects and stores food to distribute free of charge to low-income families and individuals. The goal is a nutritionally balanced supply for approximately three to seven days.

⌁Free food accessFor families and individuals experiencing hunger.
◷Every ThursdayWeekly donations also reach seniors and religious institutions.
